Buyer's Inspection

A thorough pre-purchase inspection to identify any issues before you close.

What We Inspect

  • Roof condition, flashing, gutters, and drainage
  • Foundation and structural components
  • Electrical panel, outlets, and wiring
  • HVAC systems (heating, cooling, and ventilation)
  • Plumbing, pipes, water heater, fixtures, and drainage
  • Attic insulation and ventilation
  • Windows, doors, and exterior envelope
  • Garage, driveway, and exterior structures
🏷️

Seller's Inspection (Pre-Listing Inspection)

Avoid surprises during escrow. Sell your home faster and with confidence.

Know what buyers will find before they do, and address it on your terms.

What We Inspect

  • All major systems (electrical, HVAC, plumbing)
  • Roof and exterior conditions
  • Foundation and structural integrity
  • Interior surfaces, ceilings, and walls
  • Safety hazards, GFCI, smoke detectors, CO alarms
  • Any deferred maintenance or code concerns
🔍

General Home Inspection

A complete evaluation of your home's major systems and structure, ideal for homeowners who want peace of mind.

What We Inspect

  • Full structural assessment
  • All major mechanical systems
  • Exterior and interior conditions
  • Safety items and code compliance
  • Attic, crawlspace (if accessible), and basement

What Happens After the Inspection?

You'll receive a detailed, easy-to-read report within 24 hours. No jargon, just clear, unbiased findings, including photos, and honest recommendations.
